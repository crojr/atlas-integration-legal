Privacy Policy — Atlas Integration

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. What this application is

Atlas Capema Integration is a private, internal-use integration that connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization, on behalf of CAPEMA TRADING COMPANY, S.A. It is not offered to the public and has no consumer users.

2. Data we access

Only after the company owner authorizes the connection, the application reads accounting reports and records — such as trial balance, general ledger, profit and loss, balance sheet, accounts receivable and accounts payable — through the QuickBooks Online API. OAuth tokens are issued by Intuit and stored encrypted in a private secrets manager on a private server.

3. How data is used

Data is used exclusively for internal reconciliation, audit and management reporting of the authorizing company. It is not used for advertising, profiling or marketing.

4. No AI/ML training

Data obtained from QuickBooks Online is not used to train, fine-tune or otherwise improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, and is not shared with third parties for that purpose.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It may be disclosed only when legally required by a competent authority.

6. Security

Access is protected by Intuit OAuth 2.0 tokens, stored in a secrets manager, with access restricted to authorized personnel. Credentials of individual users — logins, passwords or tokens — are never requested from or shared by users.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed for the internal purposes above. The authorizing company may revoke access at any time from the QuickBooks Online "Apps" settings, or request deletion of stored data at the contact below.
